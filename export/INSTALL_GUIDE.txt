HOW TO INSTALL ON YOUR ANDROID PHONE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------

METHOD 1: PHONE TRANSFER (RECOMMENDED)
1. Transfer "ActiDay-v1.0.0.apk" to your phone via:
   - USB cable (copy into phone's "Downloads" folder)
   - WhatsApp / Telegram (send the file to yourself)
   - Google Drive / Email attachment
2. On your phone, tap the APK file to open it.
3. Select "Install".
   (Note: If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap Settings and 
   enable "Allow from this source" for your file manager or browser).
4. Tap "Open" and enjoy ActiDay!

METHOD 2: VIA ADB / USB DEBUGGING (DEVELOPER MODE)
If your phone is connected with USB Debugging enabled:
   adb install -r ActiDay-v1.0.0.apk
